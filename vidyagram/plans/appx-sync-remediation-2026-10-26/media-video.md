## Backup and recoverability policy

Primary native video objects are in recordedvideos-vidyagram. A primary mirror is not an independent backup. This track has not verified a second video copy, retention or restore test. Shared storage backup work must establish object inventory, version/retention and a safe isolated restore target before calling recoverability complete. YouTube/externalMP4 bytes are outside the native backup inventory unless explicitly mirrored later; metadata/database backup alone cannot restore upstream bytes.

The scoped repair has protected before.ejson plus post-repair checkpoint guards. After media completes, rollback removes only the exact new four row IDs, two repair ledger docs and two new jobs, restores the original two Classes, and keeps copied storage objects recoverable. It refuses active workers or subsequent edits. See preview-and-rollback.md and execution-checkpoint.md for precise mechanisms. No rollback is currently necessary: nothing was applied.
