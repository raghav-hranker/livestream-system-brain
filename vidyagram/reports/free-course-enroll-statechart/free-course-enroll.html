Free-course enroll: who can play, and where it shows

Three things decide the outcome: the course mode the admin sets, whether the student ever tapped Enroll, and whether they hold an order. This page shows every combination, how each can change, and the three paths where the code steps outside the rules.

    The check reads the mode at courseEnrollment.ts:30-32, and the save at :33-37 doesn't read it again. The stray row grants and lists nothing while the course is Paid or Open.     Not in the model

    • A class marked free on its own, or free in its course placement. Either opens it to everyone in any mode, as before (classAccessAuthz.ts:110, 122).
    • A class placed in several courses: any course that opens it wins.
    • Course Builder chapter purchases, plan grants, retired plans, admin-deleted orders, admins, and inactive courses (Enroll answers 404).
    • Other screens that read the mode: timetable, the isEnrolled flag on cards, search hints, certificates.
    • Refunds and revoked orders.
