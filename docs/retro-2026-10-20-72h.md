## Bottlenecks, ranked by hours lost

### 1. Agent waves gated on a human "go" (≈ 39 h of the 40 h builder timeline)

The 12 implementation slices ran in four waves. Measured from `Agent` spawn to hand-back:

| Wave | Slices | Started | Hand-back | Agent time | Idle before this wave |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 01, 02 | 10-18 18:51 | 19:03 | 12 min | — |
| 2 | 03, 04, 08a | 10-19 16:08 | 16:20 | 12 min | 21 h |
| 3 | 05, 10, 11 | 10-19 23:50 | 00:15 | 25 min | 7.5 h |
| 4 | 06, 07, 08b, 09 | 10-20 10:45 | 11:01 | 16 min | 10.5 h |

Agents spent about 65 minutes implementing. The feature took 40 hours because each wave waited for you to
type "so what is next" / "go ahead". The README already encodes the dependency DAG
(`00 ∥ 01 ∥ 02 → 03 ∥ 04 ∥ 08a → 05 → 06 ∥ 07 ∥ 08b ∥ 09 ∥ 10 ∥ 11 → 12`), and it was not even followed
tightly: 10 and 11 depend only on 02 and 04, so they belonged in wave 2, not wave 3 at midnight.

**Fix:** the orchestrator runs the whole DAG unattended once the PRD is approved. Rule for
`task-implementer` orchestration: after a wave hands back, merge, run the baseline suites, and start every
task whose blockers are now merged. Stop only for HITL tasks (00, 12) or a `NEW FAILURES` suite result.
One approval at the start, one review at the end. Expected: 40 h → under 3 h for a feature of this shape.

### 2. Pricing-rule design done by chat, with the team out of the loop (3 h 15 min on 10-18)

15:33 → 18:46 in the orchestrator: 36 of your messages are "still not clear", "show me instead",
"explain graphically", "can't understand your table", with one break at 16:26 to ask the team. The
min/max/cap/revision rules were resolved, but by reading prose tables in a terminal. The artifact that
finally made it click ("show me the artifact first", 17:46) was asked for at 16:10 and arrived late.

**Fix:** when a PRD has a numeric rule (pricing, caps, weightage), the first deliverable is an interactive
artifact with sliders and a worked example, before any prose. Put the team's open questions in it and
send them the link; task 00 (team pricing answers) was HITL anyway. Write it into `to-prd`: "a pricing or
scoring rule gets a calculator artifact, not a table".

### 3. You were the relay between sessions (≈ 2 h on 10-19)

Session 068a685a: 54 messages in 3.3 h, 28 wrap-ups. A third of them are plumbing: "give me the command",
"The tunnel is open on localhost:27018. Run the dry run", "give command", "what is next". The ssh tunnel
to the Vidyagram Mongo was opened by hand because the agent's `ssh -L` was denied, so every DB step
became two turns. The same day ba195042 and 7918eec2 were also open; the four sessions never talked to
each other, so you carried the state.

**Fix:** a committed `reference/scripts/vg-tunnel.sh` (opens 27018/27019 to the Vidyagram replica,
idempotent, prints the URI) that can be allow-listed once. Then `qt-tasks.sh` style wrappers for the
import dry-run and commit. Sessions touching the same tenant should be started from one parent with
`start_session`, so hand-backs flow to it instead of through you.

### 4. Local end-to-end environment is rebuilt from scratch each time (≈ 1.5 h, 10-18 evening + 10-20)

Errors in the orchestrator: `mongod --fork` unsupported on macOS, replica set init denied, `mongodb`
