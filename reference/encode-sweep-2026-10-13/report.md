## 7. Caveats

- x264 stands in for NVENC (production records with NVENC cq23). NVENC at a given rate is typically less efficient than x264 slow, so absolute kbps transfer only as a ratio; re-run the winning points on the GPU box before shipping.
- Two 60 s windows, not a whole class. W1 is the hardest minute found by source bitrate plus eyeballing; a slide change storm or a camera cut could exceed it.
- VMAF v0.6.1 is blind to banding on the dark board gradient and is tuned for TV viewing distance; it under-weights thin text legibility. Check small-rung text by eye.
- All rungs are scored upscaled to 720p, i.e. the assumption is a phone displaying ~720p. On a 1080p laptop the low rungs look worse than these numbers say; on a 360p-sized player window they look better.
- Encode times are single-run wall-clock on an Apple M-series 10-core laptop with VMAF of the previous encode sometimes overlapping; treat ratios as +-15%.
- Baseline C uses lanczos scaling too, so the comparison isolates rate control, GOP and preset, not the scaler.
